WIZZ Priority

WIZZ Priority is a paid add-on that gives you:

  • Large trolley bag (55 × 40 × 23 cm, 10 kg) for the overhead locker
  • Priority boarding — you board first
  • Guaranteed cabin space for your trolley bag
  • Price: from ~€5–€25 depending on route and demand
  • Included in: WIZZ Plus fare bundle

Strict enforcement: Wizz Air is known for being one of the strictest airlines when it comes to carry-on baggage sizes. They use sizer boxes at the gate and frequently check bags. If your bag doesn't fit the sizer and you don't have WIZZ Priority, you will be charged €65 at the gate. It is always cheaper to buy WIZZ Priority online in advance.

About the 23 kg option

Wizz Air does not offer a 23 kg baggage option. Unlike many other airlines that use 23 kg as their standard, Wizz Air's hold luggage comes in 10 kg, 20 kg, 26 kg, and 32 kg tiers.

If you need around 23 kg, you have two options:

  • 20 kg bag: Cheaper, but you need to pack carefully. If you go over, excess weight costs €13/kg at the airport.
  • 26 kg bag: Costs a bit more (usually €6–€10 extra vs 20 kg), but gives you 6 kg of headroom. Popular choice for peace of mind.

Excess weight fee: If your checked bag weighs more than your purchased allowance, Wizz Air charges €13 per kilogram at the airport. This applies both between 10–20 kg and 20–32 kg tiers. It is almost always cheaper to buy a higher weight allowance online in advance than to pay excess weight fees at the airport.

  • Maximum per bag: 32 kg (hard limit — bags over 32 kg will not be accepted)
  • Maximum per passenger: Up to 6 hold bags
  • Size limit: 149 × 119 × 171 cm (total 439 cm)
  • Pricing varies: By route, season, demand, and how early you book
  • Online is cheaper: Always add bags during booking or via Manage Bookings — airport prices are much higher
  • No pooling: Wizz Air does not allow baggage pooling between passengers

Fees at the Gate

Wizz Air's gate fees are among the highest in Europe. The airline is notorious for strict baggage enforcement at the boarding gate.

Gate Check Fee
€65
Per Oversized Bag

If your carry-on bag doesn't fit the sizer box and you don't have WIZZ Priority, you pay €65 at the gate. The bag is then placed in the aircraft hold.

What triggers a gate fee

  • Bag too big: Your personal item doesn't fit the 40×30×20 cm sizer
  • Extra bag: You have more than one item without Priority
  • Trolley bag without Priority: You bring a wheeled bag but didn't buy WIZZ Priority
  • Bag too heavy: Less common, but weight can also be checked

How Wizz Air enforces it

  • Sizer boxes at the gate: Wizz Air places baggage sizers at boarding gates
  • Line audits: Staff may walk the boarding line checking bag sizes
  • Random checks: Not every flight, but common on busy routes
  • UK airports: £48 (equivalent to ~€65)
  • No negotiation: Gate agents have strict instructions to charge the fee

How to avoid gate fees:

  • Measure your bag at home — use a tape measure, don't guess
  • Buy WIZZ Priority online if you need a trolley bag
  • Buy a hold bag online if you have more than the free personal item
  • Wear your heaviest items (jacket, boots) — they don't count as baggage
  • Use a soft-sided bag that can be compressed to fit the sizer
  • Remember: €65 at the gate is way more expensive than €5–€25 online

Fare Types & Baggage Overview

Wizz Air offers three main fare bundles. The baggage allowance increases significantly with higher fares.

WIZZ Basic

  • 1 personal item (40×30×20 cm)
  • Trolley bag — extra cost
  • Hold luggage — extra cost
  • Seat selection — extra cost
  • Priority boarding — extra cost
  • Flight changes — fee applies

WIZZ Go

  • 1 personal item + 1 extra personal item
  • Trolley bag — extra cost
  • 1 × 20 kg hold bag
  • Seat selection (standard seats)
  • Priority boarding — extra cost
  • 30-day online check-in

WIZZ Plus

  • WIZZ Priority (trolley bag)
  • 1 × 32 kg hold bag
  • Extra legroom seat
  • Priority boarding
  • Free date changes
  • 30-day online check-in

WIZZ Priority (separate purchase)

WIZZ Priority can also be bought separately from any fare:

  • Price: from ~€5–€25 depending on route and demand
  • Includes: Large trolley bag (55×40×23 cm, 10 kg) + priority boarding
  • Guaranteed cabin space: Your trolley bag is guaranteed to fit in the cabin
  • Board first: You get to board before other passengers

Is WIZZ Go worth it? If you need a 20 kg hold bag + seat selection, compare the cost of adding these individually to a Basic fare versus the WIZZ Go bundle upgrade. On many routes, WIZZ Go works out cheaper than buying everything separately — plus you get an extra personal item and earlier check-in.

Infant & Child Baggage

Wizz Air offers baggage options for infants and children.

Infants (under 2 years, on lap)

  • Infant fare: Flat fee per flight (varies by route)
  • Carry-on: 1 additional bag for baby items (max 10 kg, fits under seat)
  • Free checked items: 1 foldable stroller + 1 car seat
  • Hold luggage: No free hold baggage — must be purchased separately
  • Baby food: Allowed in cabin in reasonable quantities

Children (2+ years, own seat)

  • Full adult baggage allowance: Same as adults in the same fare type
  • Personal item: Free (40 × 30 × 20 cm)
  • Trolley bag: Requires WIZZ Priority (paid extra or in WIZZ Plus)
  • Hold luggage: Paid extra or included in higher fares
  • Free stroller / car seat: Still allowed until age 2

Stroller policy: One fully foldable stroller per infant/child is carried free of charge, in addition to your baggage allowance. You can drop it off at the gate — it will be waiting for you at the baggage hall on arrival. Car seats are also free.

Sports Equipment & Musical Instruments

Wizz Air accepts sports equipment and musical instruments for an additional fee.

Sports equipment

  • Types accepted: Skis/snowboards, golf bags, bicycles, diving equipment, etc.
  • Fee: From ~€30 online per item (varies by route and equipment type)
  • Weight limit: Counts as hold luggage; standard weight rules apply
  • Must be booked in advance: Add during booking or via Manage Bookings
  • Proper packing required: Must be in a suitable protective case or bag
  • Bicycles: Must be properly packed / boxed; pedal and handlebar removal may be required

Musical instruments

  • Small instruments: Can be carried as cabin baggage if within personal item limits (40 × 30 × 20 cm)
  • Medium instruments: Can travel in the cabin if you buy an extra seat (must fit in the seat)
  • Large instruments: Must be checked in as hold baggage
  • Fragile items: Wizz Air has limited liability for damage to fragile items
  • Hard case recommended: Always use a hard protective case

Book sports equipment early: Sports equipment fees on Wizz Air increase as the flight fills up, just like regular hold luggage. Add your sports equipment when you book to get the lowest price. Some equipment types may have limited availability on each flight, so book early to secure space.

Other Special Items

  • Medical equipment & medication: Allowed in cabin with proper documentation; may count toward your allowance
  • Duty-free purchases: Must fit within your cabin bag allowance if bought before security
  • Assistive devices: Priority handling for passengers with reduced mobility
  • Fragile items: Must be properly packed; limited liability
  • Wedding dress: Can be carried on board if it fits within cabin bag limits, or checked in as hold luggage

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Wizz Air's carry-on baggage allowance? ▼

Every Wizz Air passenger gets one free small carry-on bag that fits under the seat:

  • Personal item (free): 40 × 30 × 20 cm, up to 10 kg — included with every fare
  • Trolley bag (WIZZ Priority): 55 × 40 × 23 cm, up to 10 kg — costs extra

The trolley bag costs from about €5–€25 when bought online as part of WIZZ Priority, but jumps to €65 if you get caught at the gate with an oversized bag.

WIZZ Priority includes:

  • Large trolley bag for the overhead locker
  • Priority boarding (board first)
  • Guaranteed cabin space for your bag

Important: Wizz Air is very strict about baggage sizes. They use sizer boxes at the gate and frequently check bags. Always measure your bag before flying.

How much does Wizz Air hold luggage cost? ▼

Wizz Air hold luggage is a paid add-on on all fares. Prices vary significantly based on route, season, demand, and how early you book.

Online prices (when added during booking):

  • 10 kg: from ~€15
  • 20 kg: from ~€20
  • 26 kg: from ~€26
  • 32 kg: from ~€32

Peak season prices can be much higher — up to €100+ per bag on popular routes in summer.

Airport prices:

Key points:

  • Maximum 6 hold bags per passenger
  • Maximum 32 kg per bag (hard limit)
  • Maximum size: 149 × 119 × 171 cm
  • Prices go up as the flight fills up — book early
  • No baggage pooling between passengers
What is the Wizz Air gate fee for oversized baggage? ▼

Wizz Air charges approximately €65 (£48 at UK airports) as a gate fee if your carry-on bag is too big and you have not purchased WIZZ Priority.

What happens at the gate:

  • Wizz Air staff use sizer boxes to check bag dimensions
  • If your bag doesn't fit, you are charged €65
  • The bag is then placed in the hold (cargo compartment)
  • No negotiation — gate agents must enforce the fee

Wizz Air is one of the strictest airlines on baggage enforcement in Europe. They frequently check bags at the gate, especially on busy flights and popular routes.

How to avoid gate fees:

  • Measure your bag at home with a tape measure
  • Buy WIZZ Priority online if you need a trolley bag
  • Buy a hold bag online if you have more than the free personal item
  • Use a soft bag that can be compressed
  • Wear your heaviest items (they don't count as baggage)

Remember: €65 at the gate is way more expensive than €5–€25 online for WIZZ Priority.

Does Wizz Air have a 23 kg baggage option? ▼

No, Wizz Air does not offer a 23 kg baggage option. Unlike many other airlines that use 23 kg as their standard checked bag weight, Wizz Air's hold luggage comes in four weight tiers:

  • 10 kg — lightest option, cheapest
  • 20 kg — most popular option
  • 26 kg — good value upgrade from 20 kg
  • 32 kg — maximum weight

If you need around 23 kg, you have two options:

  1. Buy 20 kg and pack carefully: Cheaper option, but risk of excess weight fees if you go over. Excess weight costs €13/kg at the airport, so just 3 kg over would cost €39 — more than the upgrade to 26 kg.
  2. Buy 26 kg for peace of mind: Usually only €6–€10 more than 20 kg, but gives you 6 kg of extra headroom. This is often the better value choice.

Our recommendation: If you think you might need more than 20 kg, go for the 26 kg option. The small price difference is worth it to avoid expensive excess weight fees at the airport.

Does Wizz Air weigh carry-on bags? ▼

Wizz Air's carry-on policy is primarily size-based, not weight-based. The official weight limit is 10 kg, but in practice, Wizz Air is much more focused on bag size than weight.

What Wizz Air strictly checks:

  • Size: Very strict about bag dimensions. Sizer boxes are used at the gate.
  • Number of bags: Only one personal item without WIZZ Priority.

What Wizz Air rarely checks:

  • Weight: They do not typically weigh carry-on bags at the gate. The 10 kg limit is more of a safety/handling guideline than a strictly enforced rule.

However, if your bag is clearly very heavy and the gate agent notices, they may ask you to check it. And if it does not fit in the sizer box, you will definitely be charged the €65 gate fee.

Bottom line: Focus on getting the size right. Weight is less of a concern with Wizz Air compared to airlines like JAL that strictly weigh carry-on bags. But always make sure your bag fits the 40×30×20 cm sizer for the free personal item.

How strict is Wizz Air with baggage sizes? ▼

Wizz Air is widely regarded as one of the strictest airlines in Europe when it comes to baggage size enforcement.

How they enforce:

  • Sizer boxes at gates: Wizz Air places metal sizer boxes at boarding gates
  • Line audits: Staff walk the boarding line checking bag sizes
  • Tagging system: On some routes, bags are tagged at check-in/bag drop
  • Random checks: Frequency varies by airport and route
  • No exceptions: Gate agents are instructed to charge the fee

What gets flagged:

  • Bags that are clearly too big for under the seat
  • Wheeled suitcases without WIZZ Priority
  • Passengers carrying multiple bags
  • Backpacks that look oversized

Tips to avoid problems:

  • Measure your bag at home — don't guess
  • Use a soft-sided bag that can be compressed
  • Don't overpack — a bulging bag is more likely to be checked
  • Buy WIZZ Priority if you need a trolley bag
  • Wear your jacket and heaviest items
  • Arrive early — last-minute boarding is more stressful

The €65 gate fee is a significant revenue source for Wizz Air, so they have incentive to enforce it strictly.

What is WIZZ Priority and is it worth it? ▼

WIZZ Priority is a paid add-on that gives you extra cabin baggage and boarding benefits on Wizz Air.

What's included:

  • Large trolley bag: 55 × 40 × 23 cm, up to 10 kg (for the overhead locker)
  • Priority boarding: You board the plane first
  • Guaranteed cabin space: Your trolley bag is guaranteed to fit in the cabin
  • Keep your bags with you: No risk of having your bag gate-checked

Price: From ~€5–€25 depending on route, demand, and how early you buy

How to get it:

  • Buy separately during booking or via Manage Bookings
  • Included in the WIZZ Plus fare bundle

Is it worth it?

  • Yes, if: You need a trolley bag / wheeled suitcase for the cabin. €5–€25 online is much cheaper than the €65 gate fee.
  • Yes, if: You value boarding early and having space for your bag.
  • No, if: You can travel with just a small personal item (backpack or handbag) that fits under the seat.

Compare: A trolley bag costs €65 at the gate vs. €5–€25 online for WIZZ Priority. If you need a trolley bag, it's a no-brainer — buy Priority online.
