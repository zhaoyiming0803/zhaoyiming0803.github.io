What counts as a personal item?

  • Small handbag / purse
  • Laptop bag / briefcase
  • Small backpack
  • Foldable garment bag (max. 57 × 54 × 15 cm, 8 kg)

Important: The 55 × 40 × 23 cm measurement includes wheels and handles. Lufthansa uses baggage sizers at many gates. Your cabin bag must fit in the overhead locker, and your personal item must fit under the seat in front of you.

Small musical instruments

Small musical instruments can be brought on board in place of one cabin bag, provided they do not exceed 125 cm total dimensions (height + width + depth) and 8 kg. This does not apply to Economy Basic fares on short/medium-haul flights.

Extra & Excess Baggage Fees

If you need more bags than your fare includes, or if your bags are overweight or oversized, you will pay additional fees. Prices vary significantly by route.

Additional bag (at airport) €75 – €100+ €100 – €300+
Overweight (23–32 kg) €50 €100 – €150
Oversized (158–292 cm) €50 €100 – €150
Overweight + Oversized €100 €200 – €300

Save money: Prepaying for extra baggage online can save you up to 25–50% compared to paying at the airport. Always add extra bags through Manage My Booking before you arrive at the airport.

Hard limits: No single bag can weigh more than 32 kg or measure more than 292 cm in total dimensions. Bags exceeding these limits will not be accepted as checked baggage and must be shipped as cargo.

Infant & Child Baggage

Lufthansa offers a generous baggage allowance for infants and children:

Infants (0–2 years, on lap)

  • 1 free checked bag — up to 23 kg, 158 cm total dimensions
  • 1 foldable stroller / buggy — free, can be taken to the gate
  • 1 baby car seat or bassinet — free (if used on board)
  • Exception: Not available on Economy Basic / Light fares on European routes

Children (2–11 years, own seat)

  • Same baggage allowance as adults in the same travel class
  • 1 foldable stroller can still be brought free of charge

Good to know: Strollers can be taken all the way to the boarding gate and are then placed in the hold free of charge. On long-haul flights, Lufthansa offers baby bassinets for infants — request them in advance.

Sports Equipment & Musical Instruments

Lufthansa allows you to bring sports equipment and musical instruments, with rules depending on size and your baggage allowance.

Sports Equipment

  • Within free allowance: If your sports equipment fits within your free baggage allowance (number of items + weight), it is carried free of charge (does not apply to Economy Light)
  • Exceeding allowance: Items exceeding your free baggage allowance but under 32 kg are charged at flat sports equipment rates
  • Common items: Golf clubs, skis, snowboards, surfboards, bicycles, diving equipment
  • Winter sports: One set of ski/snowboard equipment is often carried free on many routes (check your specific route)
  • Bicycles: Must be properly packed, non-electric, up to 32 kg — advance registration required

Musical Instruments

  • Small instruments (max. 125 cm total, 8 kg): Can replace one cabin bag item (except Economy Basic)
  • Larger instruments: Can be transported in the cabin on an extra seat — book under "EXTRA ITEM SEAT"
  • Large instruments (harp, double bass, etc.): Must be checked in as special baggage

Advance registration: For sports equipment and large musical instruments, always register with Lufthansa in advance (at least 24 hours before departure). This ensures there is space on the aircraft and avoids surprises at the airport.

Other Special Items

  • Folding garment bag: Up to 57 × 54 × 15 cm and 8 kg — counts as one cabin bag
  • Wheelchairs & mobility aids: Carried free of charge, in addition to your baggage allowance
  • Duty-free purchases: Must fit within your carry-on allowance
  • Medication & medical devices: Allowed in addition to carry-on, with documentation

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Lufthansa's carry-on baggage allowance? ▼

Lufthansa's carry-on allowance depends on your travel class and route:

  • Economy (most fares): 1 personal item (40 × 30 × 15 cm) + 1 cabin bag (55 × 40 × 23 cm, 8 kg)
  • Economy Basic (short/medium haul): Only 1 personal item (40 × 30 × 15 cm)
  • Business / First Class: 1 personal item + 2 cabin bags (8 kg each)

All measurements include wheels and handles. The personal item must fit under the seat, and cabin bags go in the overhead locker.

How much checked baggage does Lufthansa allow? ▼

Lufthansa's checked baggage allowance varies by class and route:

  • European routes: Economy Light/Basic = 0 bags; Economy Comfort/Flex = 1 bag (23 kg); Business = 2 bags (32 kg each)
  • Intercontinental routes: Economy Light = 0 bags; Economy Classic/Flex = 1 bag (23 kg); Premium Economy = 2 bags (23 kg each); Business = 2 bags (32 kg each); First = 3 bags (32 kg each)

All bags must not exceed 158 cm in total dimensions (length + width + height).

How much does extra baggage cost on Lufthansa? ▼

Extra baggage fees vary by route and when you pay:

  • Within Europe: Additional bags cost €50–€75 when prepaid online, more at the airport
  • Intercontinental: Additional bags cost €65–€200+ depending on route
  • Overweight (23–32 kg): €50 within Europe, €100–€150 intercontinental
  • Oversized (158–292 cm): €50 within Europe, €100–€150 intercontinental

Prepaying online saves up to 25–50% compared to airport prices. Use Lufthansa's baggage calculator for exact prices for your route.

Can I bring sports equipment on Lufthansa? ▼

Yes, you can bring sports equipment on Lufthansa. The rules depend on whether it fits within your free baggage allowance:

  • Within allowance: Free (number of items + weight must not exceed your class limit). Does not apply to Economy Light.
  • Exceeds allowance: Charged at flat sports equipment rates, max 32 kg per item
  • Common items: Golf clubs, skis/snowboards, surfboards, bicycles, diving equipment
  • Winter sports: One ski/snowboard set is often free on many routes

Always register sports equipment in advance (at least 24 hours before departure).

What is Lufthansa's infant baggage allowance? ▼

Infants (0–2 years) traveling on an adult's lap are entitled to:

  • 1 free checked bag — up to 23 kg, 158 cm total dimensions
  • 1 foldable stroller / buggy — free, can be taken to the gate
  • 1 baby car seat or bassinet — free if used on board

This applies to most fares, but not to Economy Basic / Light fares on European routes. Children over 2 have the same baggage allowance as adults in the same class.

Is a backpack considered a personal item on Lufthansa? ▼

Yes, a small backpack can count as your personal item on Lufthansa, as long as it fits within the 40 × 30 × 15 cm dimensions and fits under the seat in front of you.

If your backpack is larger than 40 × 30 × 15 cm, it will count as your cabin bag (55 × 40 × 23 cm, 8 kg) — and you won't be able to bring an additional cabin bag. Make sure your bag fits within the size for the category you intend it for.

Does Lufthansa weigh carry-on bags? ▼

Lufthansa does weigh carry-on bags at some airports, especially on busy routes or if your bag appears heavy. The 8 kg weight limit for cabin bags is enforced.

Personal items (40 × 30 × 15 cm) are less likely to be weighed, but they must fit under the seat. Lufthansa also uses baggage sizers to check dimensions at the gate. If your bag is too big or too heavy, you may have to check it in and pay excess baggage fees.

What is Economy Basic on Lufthansa? ▼

Economy Basic is Lufthansa's cheapest fare, available on short and medium-haul European routes. It includes:

  • Only 1 personal item (40 × 30 × 15 cm) — no cabin bag allowed
  • No checked baggage
  • No seat selection (assigned at check-in)
  • Non-flexible ticket

If you want to bring a cabin bag, you need to book at least Economy Light or pay to add a cabin bag. Be careful — many travelers are caught out by the no-cabin-bag rule on Basic fares.
