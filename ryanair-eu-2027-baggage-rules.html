Ryanair EU 2027 Baggage Rules:
What Will Change

Under the new EU air passenger rights regulation, Ryanair must include a free wheeled cabin bag in every base fare starting in 2027. Here is what we know so far.

Original Content: EU Free Cabin Bag Rules 2027 – New Carry-on Law Explained

Quick answer: Yes — starting in 2027, Ryanair passengers flying from EU airports will get a free wheeled cabin bag (up to 7 kg) included in the base fare, in addition to a personal item under the seat.

Current Ryanair Baggage Policy (2026)

Right now, Ryanair's basic fare only includes one small personal item that must fit under the seat:

What Changes in 2027

Under the new EU regulation, Ryanair's base fare (the cheapest ticket) must include:

1️⃣ Personal Item

40 × 30 × 15 cm
Free under the seat. This is slightly larger than Ryanair's current 40×20×25 cm under-seat limit.

2️⃣ Wheeled Cabin Bag

Up to 7 kg
Free in the overhead bin. This is the big change — currently you pay extra for this on basic fares.

Will Ryanair Raise Prices?

Almost certainly, yes. Ryanair's business model relies on "unbundling" — selling a cheap base ticket and then charging extra for everything else, including cabin bags. If the EU forces them to include a cabin bag for free, they will likely raise base fares to compensate.

What to expect: The base fare will probably go up, but the total cost (ticket + cabin bag) may end up similar to what you pay today when you add a bag during booking. The main benefit is transparency — you won't be surprised by hidden fees at booking or at the gate.

Ryanair's Position

Ryanair has not yet released a detailed official response to the final regulation. However, the airline has historically opposed mandatory free cabin bags, arguing that it would raise prices for all passengers and reduce choice.

easyJet's CEO has publicly called the rule "crazy European legislation," suggesting the low-cost industry is not happy with the change.

What This Means for You

  • If you always pay for a cabin bag: The total cost may stay about the same, but it will be built into the ticket price — no more surprise add-ons.
  • If you only travel with a small bag: You may end up paying more, since the base fare will likely increase to cover the "free" cabin bag you don't use.
  • If you fly non-EU routes: The rule only applies to flights departing from EU airports. Ryanair flights outside the EU (e.g., domestic UK, Morocco, etc.) are not affected.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will my 2027 Ryanair booking include a free cabin bag? ▼

If the flight departs from an EU airport and the travel date is after the regulation takes effect (expected second half of 2027), then yes — the base fare must include a free cabin bag.

What size will the free cabin bag be? ▼

The EU rule sets a weight limit of 7 kg and a maximum linear dimension. Ryanair may set its own specific dimensions as long as they meet or exceed the EU minimum. Check our 100cm 7kg rule guide for details on how the size limit works.

Does this apply to Ryanair flights from the UK? ▼

The EU regulation applies to flights departing from EU airports. UK domestic flights and UK-to-non-EU flights on Ryanair are not covered. However, flights from the UK to the EU on Ryanair may be affected depending on the final scope of the regulation.

Will priority boarding still exist? ▼

Probably yes. The rule only requires a free cabin bag — it doesn't say anything about boarding priority. Ryanair will likely still sell priority boarding as a separate add-on.
