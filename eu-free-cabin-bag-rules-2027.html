EU Free Cabin Bag Rules 2027:
Everything You Need to Know

Starting in 2027, every EU flight must include a free cabin bag and personal item in the base fare. Here is what changes, who is affected, and what it means for your luggage.

Original Content: EU Free Cabin Bag Rules 2027 – New Carry-on Law Explained

What Is the New EU Rule?

In June 2026, the European Parliament and Council reached a final agreement on revising the EU Air Passenger Rights Regulation (261/2004). The revised regulation requires that all airlines operating flights from and within the EU must include two free items in the base fare:

📦 Personal Item

40 × 30 × 15 cm
Must fit under the seat in front of you. Backpacks, laptop bags, and small handbags usually qualify.

🧳 Cabin Bag (Wheeled)

Up to 7 kg
Must fit in the overhead bin. Size measured as total linear dimensions (length + width + height).

📅 Effective Date

2027
Expected to apply from the second half of 2027. Airlines have a transition period to adjust.

Key takeaway: Budget airlines like Ryanair, easyJet, and Wizz Air can no longer charge extra for a standard wheeled carry-on bag. The "basic fare only gets a small bag" model is ending for all EU flights.

Who Is Affected?

The rule applies to all flights departing from an EU airport, regardless of where the airline is based. This includes:

  • EU-based airlines: Lufthansa, Air France, KLM, Ryanair, easyJet, Wizz Air, etc.
  • Non-EU airlines flying from the EU: Turkish Airlines, Emirates, Qatar Airways, etc. — but only on their EU departure flights

Airlines Most Impacted

Low-cost carriers that currently charge separately for cabin bags will see the biggest change:

Airline Current Basic Fare 2027 Change
Ryanair 1 small bag only (40×20×25 cm) + Free wheeled cabin bag (7 kg)
easyJet 1 small bag only (45×36×20 cm) + Free wheeled cabin bag (7 kg)
Wizz Air 1 small bag only (40×30×20 cm) + Free wheeled cabin bag (7 kg)
Vueling 1 small bag only (40×20×30 cm) + Free wheeled cabin bag (7 kg)
Transavia 1 small bag only (40×30×20 cm) + Free wheeled cabin bag (7 kg)

Note: Full-service airlines (Lufthansa, Air France, KLM, etc.) already include a cabin bag in their basic economy fare, so the impact on their passengers will be less dramatic. The biggest change is for budget airline passengers.

What About the 100 cm Rule?

The new EU rule sets a maximum total linear dimension for the free wheeled cabin bag. This means the sum of length + width + height must not exceed the specified limit. Regulation enters into force. Airlines have a transition period to comply with the new baggage requirements.

Airline Reactions

The new rule has been controversial among budget airlines. easyJet's CEO publicly criticized it as "crazy European legislation," arguing that it will force airlines to raise base ticket prices, ultimately making travel more expensive for price-sensitive passengers.

Ryanair and Wizz Air have not yet announced detailed plans for how they will adjust their fare structures, but industry analysts expect base fares to increase to compensate for the lost baggage revenue.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this apply to flights within the EU only?

The rule applies to all flights departing from an EU airport, whether domestic, intra-EU, or long-haul. It also applies to flights arriving in the EU on EU-based carriers.

Will ticket prices go up?

Most analysts expect base fares on budget airlines to increase, since the cost of the cabin bag will be built into the ticket price. However, the total cost (fare + bag) may end up similar to what passengers pay today when they add a bag separately.

What if my bag is bigger than the limit?

Airlines can still charge for oversized or overweight bags. The rule only guarantees that a bag within the specified size and weight limits is included for free. Bags that exceed the limits may still incur fees at the gate.

Does this apply to basic economy fares?

Yes. The rule requires the free cabin bag to be included in the base fare — the cheapest available ticket. This is the key change that ends the budget airline model of charging extra for a wheeled carry-on.

When exactly does it take effect?

The regulation is expected to take effect in the second half of 2027. The exact date will be specified in the final published text of the regulation. Airlines will have a transition period to adjust their systems and fare structures.
