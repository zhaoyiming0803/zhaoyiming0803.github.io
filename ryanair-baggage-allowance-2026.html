Free Carry-On Bag (All Passengers)

Every Ryanair passenger, regardless of fare type, gets one free small personal bag that must fit under the seat in front of you.

Free bag dimensions: 40 × 30 × 20 cm (including wheels and handles)
Weight limit: No official weight limit (but must fit under seat)
Placement: Under the seat in front of you

What counts as a small personal bag?

  • Backpacks
  • Handbags / purses
  • Laptop bags
  • Small tote bags
  • Duty-free shopping bags

Important: The 40 × 30 × 20 cm measurement must include wheels and handles. Ryanair uses baggage sizers at the gate, and if your bag does not fit, you will have to check it in at the gate for a higher fee.

Priority & 2 Cabin Bags

If you want to bring a larger wheeled cabin bag on board, you need to purchase Priority & 2 Cabin Bags. This is the most popular add-on for Ryanair passengers.

What's included with Priority?

  • ✅ 1 small personal bag (40 × 30 × 20 cm) — under seat
  • ✅ 1 cabin bag (55 × 40 × 20 cm, 10 kg) — overhead locker
  • ✅ Priority boarding (board first via Priority queue)

Price tip: Priority is cheapest when purchased at the time of booking. Prices increase if added later through Manage My Booking, and it cannot be purchased at the gate. If you arrive at the gate with a cabin bag but no Priority, you will pay a gate fee (around €50-€70) to have it placed in the hold.

Airport vs. Online Prices

Buying your checked bag at the airport is significantly more expensive than booking online:

  • 10kg bag at airport desk: €35.99 – €40
  • 20kg bag at airport desk: €39.99 – €59.99
  • Gate fee (unpaid cabin bag): €50 – €70+

Gate fees are the most expensive option. If you bring a cabin bag to the gate without Priority boarding, Ryanair will charge you a gate fee to put it in the hold — this is almost always more expensive than buying Priority or a checked bag in advance.

Infant Baggage

Infants (under 2 years old) traveling on an adult's lap have a specific baggage allowance:

  • No cabin bag for the infant themselves
  • 5kg baby bag — the accompanying adult can carry an additional baby bag weighing up to 5kg
  • 2 free baby equipment items — checked in for free, such as:
    • Stroller / pushchair
    • Car seat
    • Travel cot
    • High chair

Good to know: Strollers can be taken all the way to the boarding gate and are then placed in the hold free of charge. You do not need to check them in at the bag drop desk.

Sports Equipment & Musical Instruments

Ryanair allows you to bring sports equipment and musical instruments, with specific rules for each category.

Sports Equipment

  • Small items that fit within your cabin baggage allowance can be brought on board for free
  • Large sports equipment must be checked in for a fee, with a 20kg weight limit per item
  • Bicycles have a higher weight limit of 30kg, but must be properly protected (non-electric only)
  • Examples of checked sports equipment: golf clubs, skis, snowboards, surfboards, fishing rods, hockey equipment

Musical Instruments

  • Small instruments that fit within your cabin baggage allowance can be brought on board for free
  • Larger instruments (guitars, violins) that exceed cabin dimensions can travel on an extra seat — book under "EXTRA ITEM SEAT"
  • Large instruments (harp, double bass, drums) must be checked in for a fee, 20kg limit

Note: Sports equipment and musical instrument fees are separate from your standard checked baggage allowance. They are charged per item and do not count toward your 20kg/23kg bag limit.

Excess & Overweight Baggage

If your checked bag exceeds the weight you paid for, you will be charged excess baggage fees:

  • Excess weight fee: €11 per kg (at the airport)
  • Maximum weight per bag: 32 kg (hard limit — cannot exceed this)
  • Oversized bags at the gate: €50 – €70+ gate fee to place in the hold

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Ryanair's free carry-on bag size in 2026?

Ryanair's free carry-on bag (small personal bag) must be no larger than 40 × 30 × 20 cm, including wheels and handles. There is no official weight limit, but it must fit under the seat in front of you. This is included with every fare type.

Ryanair uses baggage sizers at many gates, so make sure your bag fits within these dimensions to avoid gate fees.

How much does Priority & 2 Cabin Bags cost?

Priority & 2 Cabin Bags costs between €12 and €35.99 when purchased at the time of booking, depending on the route and travel dates. Prices are higher if added after booking through Manage My Booking.

It includes:

  • 1 small personal bag (40 × 30 × 20 cm) — under seat
  • 1 cabin bag (55 × 40 × 20 cm, 10 kg) — overhead locker
  • Priority boarding (board first)

What are Ryanair's checked baggage fees?

Ryanair offers three checked baggage options:

  • 10kg Check-in Bag: €9.49 – €44.99 online (1 per passenger)
  • 20kg Check-in Bag: €18.99 – €59.99 online (up to 3 per passenger)
  • 23kg Check-in Bag: Higher price (1 per passenger)

Prices vary by route and travel dates. Buying at the airport is significantly more expensive — 10kg bags cost €35.99–€40 at the desk, and gate fees for unpaid cabin bags can be €50–€70+.

Can I bring sports equipment on Ryanair?

Yes, you can bring sports equipment on Ryanair. The rules depend on the size and type of equipment:

  • Small items that fit within your cabin baggage allowance are free to bring on board
  • Large sports equipment (golf clubs, skis, surfboards, etc.) must be checked in for a fee, with a 20kg weight limit per item
  • Bicycles have a 30kg weight limit, must be non-electric, and properly protected

Sports equipment fees are separate from your standard checked baggage allowance.

What is the baggage allowance for infants?

Infants (under 2 years old) traveling on an adult's lap do not get their own cabin baggage allowance. However, the accompanying adult can bring:

  • 1 additional baby bag weighing up to 5kg
  • 2 items of baby equipment checked in for free (stroller, car seat, travel cot, high chair, etc.)

Strollers can be taken to the boarding gate and then placed in the hold free of charge.

Does Ryanair weigh the free small bag?

Ryanair does not officially state a weight limit for the free small personal bag, and they generally do not weigh it. However, they do check the size using baggage sizers at the gate.

That said, the bag must be small enough to fit under the seat in front of you, and staff can use their discretion if a bag appears excessively heavy or oversized.

Can I bring a laptop bag plus a backpack?

No — with a standard (non-Priority) ticket, you are only allowed one small personal bag. You cannot bring both a laptop bag and a backpack as separate items.

You have two options:

  • Put your laptop inside your backpack so you only have one bag
  • Purchase Priority & 2 Cabin Bags, which allows two bags (small bag + cabin bag)

What happens if my bag is too big at the gate?

If your bag does not fit in the baggage sizer at the gate and you do not have Priority boarding, Ryanair will:

  • Place your bag in the aircraft hold (cargo)
  • Charge you a gate fee of approximately €50–€70+

This is the most expensive way to check a bag. It is always cheaper to buy Priority or a checked bag in advance online.
