Will Ryanair Carry-on Be Free in 2027?

Short answer: Yes — for flights departing from EU airports. Here is the full breakdown of what changes, when, and what it will cost.

Original Content: EU Free Cabin Bag Rules 2027 – New Carry-on Law Explained

Yes, Ryanair carry-on will be free in 2027 for all flights departing from EU airports. The new EU air passenger rights regulation requires every base fare to include a free wheeled cabin bag (up to 7 kg) plus a personal item under the seat.

The Short Answer

Starting in 2027, if you book a Ryanair flight departing from any EU airport, your base fare ticket will automatically include:

  • ✅ 1 personal item (40 × 30 × 15 cm, under seat)
  • ✅ 1 wheeled cabin bag (up to 7 kg, overhead bin)

No extra fee. No add-on to buy at checkout. It is included in the price of the ticket.

Why Is This Changing?

The EU is updating its Air Passenger Rights Regulation (261/2004). The revised regulation, agreed in June 2026 and approved by the Council in July 2026, bans the practice of charging separately for a standard cabin bag on EU flights.

The goal is transparency — passengers should be able to compare ticket prices knowing that a reasonable amount of luggage is included, not hidden behind add-on fees.

What About the Price?

Here is the catch: "free" doesn't mean the total cost will necessarily go down.

Today (2026) 2027 (Expected)
Base fare €9.99 – €49.99 Likely higher
Cabin bag fee €8 – €35 extra Included (free)
Total with bag €18 – €85 About the same?

Reality check: Ryanair will almost certainly raise base fares to cover the cost of the "free" cabin bag. The total cost of flying with a bag may not change much — but the price you see at first glance will be more honest.

Who Benefits Most?

  • People who forget to add a bag: No more €50 gate fees because you didn't realize the basic fare didn't include a wheeled bag.
  • Price comparers: You can compare fares across airlines knowing a cabin bag is included in every price.
  • Occasional flyers: If you only fly once or twice a year, you won't have to learn the ins and outs of Ryanair's baggage system.

Who Might Lose Out?

  • Ultra-light packers: If you always travel with just a backpack and never pay for a cabin bag, your base fare may go up even though you don't use the "free" bag.
  • Budget travelers chasing the absolute lowest price: The €9.99 "no bag" fare may disappear, replaced by a higher base fare that includes the bag.

When Does It Start?

The regulation is expected to take effect in the second half of 2027. The exact date will be published in the Official Journal of the European Union.

Ryanair will have a transition period to update their booking systems, website, and airport procedures.

What Size Bag Will Be Free?

The EU rule sets a standard for the minimum size and weight that must be included:

  • Personal item: 40 × 30 × 15 cm (must fit under seat)
  • Cabin bag: Up to 7 kg, with maximum total dimensions (length + width + height)

For a detailed explanation of the size limit and whether your current suitcase will qualify, see our EU 100cm 7kg Carry-on Rule Explained guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Ryanair carry-on really going to be free? ▼

Yes — but "free" means included in the base fare. The ticket price itself will likely go up. You won't pay a separate bag fee anymore, but the overall ticket price will probably be higher than today's basic no-bag fare.

Does this apply to all Ryanair flights? ▼

No. It applies to flights departing from EU airports. Ryanair flights outside the EU (e.g., domestic UK, Morocco, Turkey routes) are not covered by this EU regulation.

Will my 10kg cabin bag still be free? ▼

The EU rule sets a minimum of 7 kg. Ryanair could choose to allow more (they currently offer 10 kg on Plus fares), but they are only required to allow 7 kg for free. Check back closer to 2027 for the exact details.

Can I still pay for priority boarding? ▼

Most likely yes. The regulation only requires a free cabin bag — it doesn't regulate boarding priority. Ryanair will probably still sell priority boarding as a separate add-on service.

What if I already booked a 2027 flight? ▼

If your flight is after the regulation takes effect, the new rules should apply — even if you booked earlier. Airlines are required to comply with the law in effect on the date of travel, not the date of booking.
