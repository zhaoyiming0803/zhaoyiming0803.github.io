Carry-On Baggage

Air Peace allows one piece of hand luggage plus a small personal item on all flights. The weight limit depends on your route and cabin class.

Route / Class Max Size Weight Limit Personal Item
Economy Domestic / Regional 45 × 33 × 20 cm 6 kg 1 small item
Business Domestic / Regional 45 × 33 × 20 cm 10 kg 1 small item
Economy International 45 × 33 × 20 cm 7 kg + handbag 1 small handbag
Business International 45 × 33 × 20 cm 10–14 kg 1 personal item

Personal items allowed

In addition to your carry-on bag, you may also bring one small personal item that fits under the seat in front:

  • Laptop bag / briefcase
  • Handbag / purse
  • Small backpack
  • Coat or wrap
  • Umbrella
  • Reading material
  • Infant food for consumption on board
  • Walking stick / crutches / mobility aids

Strict enforcement: Air Peace is known for strictly enforcing carry-on weight limits at the gate, especially on domestic Nigerian routes. If your bag is overweight, you will be required to check it and pay excess baggage fees at the airport rate.

Checked Baggage: Three Systems

Air Peace uses three different baggage systems depending on your route. The allowance, pricing model, and fees are all different.

Domestic
Routes: Within Nigeria (Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, etc.)

Weight concept — total weight limit:

  • Economy: 20 kg total
  • Business: 30 kg total
  • Max per bag: 32 kg
  • Excess: ~₦1,000/kg
Regional
Routes: West Africa (Accra, Dakar, Banjul, Monrovia, etc.)

Weight concept — total weight limit:

  • Economy: 30 kg standard
  • Business: 40 kg standard

  • Max per bag: 32 kg
International
Routes: London, Dubai, Johannesburg, Mumbai, Guangzhou

Piece concept — number of bags:

  • Economy: 2 × 23 kg
  • Business: 2 × 32 kg
  • Dubai: 2 × 25 kg (Economy)
  • Max per bag: 32 kg / 160 cm

Domestic Routes (Nigeria)

On domestic flights within Nigeria, Air Peace uses a weight concept system — you get a total weight allowance that can be distributed across any number of bags.

Cabin Class Free Checked Weight Max Per Bag Excess Fee
Economy Class 20 kg total 32 kg ~₦1,000 / kg
Business Class 30 kg total 32 kg ~₦1,000 / kg
  • Weight concept: Total weight across all bags counts toward your allowance
  • Baggage pooling: Allowed for passengers on the same booking
  • Excess baggage: Paid per kilogram at the airport
  • Online discount: Pre-purchase online for lower rates (when available)

Regional Routes (West Africa)

On regional flights within West Africa, Air Peace also uses a weight concept system, but with higher allowances than domestic routes.

Route-specific allowances: Some regional routes have higher baggage allowances than the standard 30kg Economy / 40kg Business. Always check your specific route's allowance before you fly — Lagos-Dakar gets a generous 46kg in Economy!

International Routes (Long-Haul)

On international long-haul flights (London, Dubai, Johannesburg, Mumbai, Guangzhou), Air Peace uses a piece concept system — you are allowed a specific number of bags, each with its own weight limit.

Route / Class Free Bags Weight Per Bag Max Size (per bag)
Economy Standard 2 pieces 23 kg each 160 cm (L+W+H)
Economy Dubai 2 pieces 25 kg each 160 cm (L+W+H)
Business All routes 2 pieces 32 kg each 160 cm (L+W+H)
  • Piece concept: Each bag counts as one piece, regardless of weight (up to the limit)
  • Baggage pooling: Allowed within the same booking reference
  • Maximum per bag: 32 kg / 70 lbs (all cabins)
  • Maximum dimensions: 160 cm total (length + width + height)
  • Extra weight (1-9 kg): Can be purchased per kg on piece routes (Economy only)

Hard limit: No single bag may weigh more than 32 kg on any Air Peace route. If your bag exceeds 32 kg, you must split the contents into multiple bags. Bags over 32 kg will not be accepted as checked baggage.

✈ London ↔ Lagos Route: Baggage & Cost

The London Gatwick / Heathrow ↔ Lagos / Abuja route is Air Peace's flagship international route. Here is everything you need to know about baggage costs on this route:

  • Economy Class: 2 pieces × 23 kg (46 kg total)
  • Business Class: 2 pieces × 32 kg (64 kg total)
  • Carry-on Economy: 7 kg + small handbag
  • Carry-on Business: 10–14 kg

Excess Baggage Fees (London Route):

  • First extra bag (online): USD 150 (Lagos→London) / GBP 100 (London→Lagos)
  • First extra bag (airport): USD 200 (Lagos→London) / GBP 150 (London→Lagos)
  • Extra kg (1–9 kg): USD 20/kg (Lagos→London) / GBP 15/kg (London→Lagos)
  • Progressive pricing: Each additional bag increases by USD 50

Fare Types & Baggage Overview

Air Peace is a full-service airline — every fare includes checked baggage. There is no "basic economy" with zero bags like some European or American carriers.

Economy Class

Most popular

  • Domestic: 20 kg checked
  • Regional: 30 kg checked
  • International: 2 × 23 kg
  • Carry-on: 6–7 kg
  • Meal included (intl)

Business Class

Premium experience

  • Domestic: 30 kg checked
  • Regional: 40 kg checked
  • International: 2 × 32 kg
  • Carry-on: 10–14 kg
  • Priority boarding + lounge

Good to know: Unlike many airlines, Air Peace includes checked baggage in all fare types — even the cheapest Economy ticket gets free checked bags. No need to pay extra for a bag on top of your fare, which is common on low-cost carriers.

Infant & Child Baggage

Air Peace offers baggage allowances for infants and children.

Infants (0–2 years, on lap)

  • Infant fare: 10% of adult ticket price (all classes)
  • Free checked baggage: 10 kg total
  • Can be checked or carry-on: Infant luggage can be checked in or used as carry-on
  • Additional free items: Diaper bag, collapsible stroller
  • No separate seat: Infant sits on adult's lap

Children (2–11 years, own seat)

  • Full adult baggage allowance: Same as adults in the same travel class
  • Same carry-on allowance: Same as adults
  • Child fare: Discounted fare (varies by route)
  • Unaccompanied minors: Available for ages 5–15, fee applies (USD 80–100)

Stroller policy: One collapsible stroller per child is carried free of charge on all Air Peace routes, in addition to the normal baggage allowance. Strollers can be checked in at the gate.

Sports Equipment & Musical Instruments

Air Peace accepts sports equipment and musical instruments on most routes, but advance approval is usually required.

Sports Equipment

  • Types accepted: Golf bags, surfboards, bicycles, diving equipment, etc.
  • Prior approval required: Must contact Air Peace in advance
  • May count toward allowance: If within weight/size limits, may be included
  • Additional fees: Oversized or overweight items incur extra charges
  • Proper packing: Must be in a suitable protective case

Musical Instruments

  • Small instruments: May be carried as cabin baggage if within size/weight limits
  • Medium instruments: Can travel in the cabin if you buy an extra seat ("Extra Seat")
  • Large instruments: Must be checked in as hold baggage
  • Prior approval: Recommended for all instruments
  • Packing tip: Use a hard-shell case for maximum protection

Always call ahead: Sports equipment and musical instruments on Air Peace often require advance booking and approval. Contact Air Peace customer service or your travel agent well before your flight to avoid being turned away at the airport.

Other Special Items

  • Wheelchairs & mobility aids: Carried free of charge, in addition to your baggage allowance
  • Medical equipment & medication: Allowed in addition to carry-on, with proper documentation
  • Duty-free purchases: Must fit within your carry-on allowance
  • Wedding dress: Can be carried on board if it fits within cabin bag limits, or checked in
  • Assistive devices: Priority handling for passengers with reduced mobility

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Air Peace's carry-on baggage allowance? ▼

Air Peace carry-on allowance depends on your route and cabin class:

Domestic / Regional flights:

  • Economy: 6 kg
  • Business: 10 kg

International flights:

  • Economy: 7 kg + small handbag
  • Business: 10–14 kg

All cabin bags must not exceed 45 × 33 × 20 cm. You may also bring a small personal item like a laptop bag, handbag, or small backpack that fits under the seat in front.

Note: Air Peace strictly enforces carry-on weight limits, especially on domestic routes. Overweight bags will be checked and charged excess fees.

How much checked baggage does Air Peace allow? ▼

Air Peace has three different baggage systems depending on your route:

Domestic (Nigeria) — weight concept:

  • Economy: 20 kg total
  • Business: 30 kg total

Regional (West Africa) — weight concept:

  • Economy: 30 kg standard (up to 46 kg on some routes)
  • Business: 40 kg standard (up to 64 kg on some routes)

International (London, Dubai, etc.) — piece concept:

  • Economy: 2 pieces × 23 kg (Dubai: 2 × 25 kg)
  • Business: 2 pieces × 32 kg

Maximum per bag: 32 kg. Maximum dimensions: 160 cm (L+W+H) on international routes.

How much does excess baggage cost on Air Peace? ▼

Excess baggage fees on Air Peace vary by route type:

Domestic / Regional (weight concept):

  • Domestic Nigeria: ~₦1,000 per kg at the airport
  • Regional: varies by route

International (piece concept):

  • First extra bag (Economy): USD 100 online
  • First extra bag (Business): USD 130 online
  • Progressive pricing: each additional bag + USD 50
  • Airport rates are higher

London route specifics:

  • Extra bag online: USD 150 (Lagos→London) / GBP 100 (London→Lagos)
  • Extra bag airport: USD 200 / GBP 150
  • Extra kg (1-9kg): USD 20/kg / GBP 15/kg

Always buy extra baggage online — it is significantly cheaper than airport rates.

What is Air Peace's infant baggage allowance? ▼

Infants (under 2 years) traveling on an adult's lap on Air Peace:

  • Infant fare: 10% of the adult ticket price
  • Free checked baggage: 10 kg total
  • Can be checked or carry-on: Infant luggage can be either checked in or used as carry-on
  • Additional free items: Diaper bag, collapsible stroller / pushchair

Children aged 2 and over with their own seat get the full adult baggage allowance for their travel class.

Unaccompanied minor service is available for children ages 5–15 for a fee of approximately USD 80–100.

Does Air Peace weigh carry-on bags? ▼

Yes, Air Peace does weigh carry-on bags at many airports, especially on domestic Nigerian routes. The 6 kg (Economy) and 10 kg (Business) limits are enforced.

If your carry-on bag is overweight or too large, you will be required to check it in and may have to pay excess baggage fees at the airport rate, which is more expensive than pre-purchasing online.

On international routes, enforcement may be slightly more relaxed, but you should still pack within the limits to avoid unexpected charges.

It is always better to pack within the limits or pre-purchase extra checked baggage online to save money.

What is the maximum weight for a single bag on Air Peace? ▼

32 kg is the absolute maximum weight for any single checked bag on Air Peace, regardless of cabin class or route.

If your bag weighs more than 32 kg, you must split the contents into multiple bags. Bags over 32 kg will not be accepted as checked baggage — they must be shipped as cargo instead.

Note: The standard free allowance varies by route and class. On international routes, Economy bags are limited to 23 kg each. If your bag is 23–32 kg, you will pay excess baggage fees (either per kg or per piece depending on the route).

Maximum dimensions per bag: 160 cm total (length + width + height) on international routes.

Can I bring sports equipment on Air Peace? ▼

Yes, Air Peace accepts sports equipment on most routes, but there are important things to know:

  • Prior approval required: You must contact Air Peace in advance to arrange sports equipment transport
  • Types accepted: Golf bags, surfboards, bicycles, diving equipment, and more
  • May count toward allowance: If the equipment fits within your weight/size limits, it may be included in your free allowance
  • Additional fees: Oversized or overweight items will incur extra charges
  • Proper packing required: All sports equipment must be in a suitable protective case

Always call Air Peace customer service well before your flight to confirm availability, fees, and any special requirements for your specific sports equipment.
