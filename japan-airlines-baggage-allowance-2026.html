Two baggage systems: Japan Airlines operates two different baggage systems — domestic (Japan, weight concept) and international (piece concept). The rules are very different — make sure you know which applies to your flight. If your domestic flight is part of an international ticket, international rules apply.

Carry-On Baggage

Japan Airlines allows one piece of carry-on baggage plus one personal item on all flights. The total combined weight of both items must not exceed 10 kg.

Item Max Size Weight Limit Notes
Carry-on bag 55 × 40 × 25 cm Part of 10 kg total 115 cm total (L+W+H)
Personal item Fits under seat Part of 10 kg total Handbag, laptop bag, etc.
Total combined — 10 kg max Both items together

Personal items allowed

In addition to your carry-on bag, you may bring one personal item that fits under the seat in front:

  • Handbag / purse
  • Laptop bag / briefcase
  • Small backpack
  • Camera bag
  • Coat or wrap
  • Umbrella
  • Reading material
  • Infant food for consumption on board
  • Walking stick / crutches / mobility aids

JAL weighs carry-on bags: Japan Airlines is known for strictly enforcing carry-on weight limits. The 10 kg combined limit is often checked at the gate, especially on busy flights. If your bags are overweight, you will be required to check them and pay excess baggage fees at the airport rate.

Checked Baggage: Domestic vs International

Japan Airlines uses two completely different baggage systems depending on whether you are flying domestically within Japan or on an international route.

Domestic
Routes: Within Japan (Tokyo, Osaka, Sapporo, Fukuoka, etc.)

Weight concept — total weight limit:

  • Economy / Class J: 20 kg total
  • First Class: 45 kg total
  • Unlimited number of bags
  • Max per bag: 32 kg
  • Size: 50 × 60 × 120 cm
International
Routes: All flights to/from Japan (US, Europe, Asia, etc.)

Piece concept — number of bags:

  • Economy: 2 × 23 kg
  • Premium Economy: 2 × 23 kg
  • Business: 3 × 32 kg
  • First: 3 × 32 kg
  • Max per bag: 45 kg / 203 cm

Important: If your domestic Japan flight is included in an international ticket (e.g., you fly JFK-NRT and then connect to HND-ITM on the same ticket), international baggage rules apply to the domestic segment as well. You get the piece-concept allowance, not the domestic weight allowance.

Domestic Flights (Japan)

On domestic flights within Japan, JAL uses a weight concept system — you get a total weight allowance that can be distributed across any number of bags.

Extra & Excess Baggage Fees

JAL excess baggage fees depend on your route and the type of excess (extra piece, overweight, or oversize). These fees can stack — a bag that is both extra and overweight incurs both charges.

International Routes: Excess Fees

Type of Excess Japan ↔ Asia / Guam / Oceania Japan ↔ Americas / Europe / Middle East / Africa
Additional bag (per piece) JPY 10,000 (~USD 100) JPY 20,000 (~USD 200)
Overweight 23–32 kg (per piece) JPY 10,000 (~USD 100) JPY 10,000 (~USD 100)
Overweight 32–45 kg (per piece) JPY 60,000 (~USD 600) JPY 60,000 (~USD 600)
Oversize (over 203 cm) JPY 20,000 (~USD 200) JPY 20,000 (~USD 200)

Fees stack: Excess baggage fees are cumulative. For example, if you bring an extra bag that is also overweight (23-32 kg) and oversize on a US route, you would pay: USD 200 (extra bag) + USD 100 (overweight) + USD 200 (oversize) = USD 500 total for that one bag.

  • Airport payment: Excess baggage fees are paid at the check-in counter

  • Maximum total: 100 kg on mainline aircraft, 45 kg on small aircraft

Save money: JAL excess baggage fees are expensive, especially on international routes. It is often cheaper to pack lighter, ship items ahead, or upgrade your fare class to get a higher allowance. JMB elite members get extra baggage benefits.

Infant & Child Baggage

Japan Airlines offers baggage allowances for infants and children.

Infants (0–2 years, on lap)

  • Infant fare: Typically 10% of adult fare (international)
  • International: 1 checked bag (up to 115 cm total dimensions)
  • Domestic: No separate checked baggage allowance
  • Additional free items: 1 foldable stroller, 1 baby carrier, 1 car seat
  • Carry-on: Baby food, formula, and sterilized water allowed in reasonable quantities
  • Bassinet: Available on international flights (request in advance)

Children (2–11 years, own seat)

  • Full adult baggage allowance: Same as adults in the same travel class
  • Same carry-on allowance: 10 kg total (2 items)
  • Child fare: Discounted fare (varies by route)
  • Unaccompanied minors: Available for ages 5–11, fee applies

Stroller policy: One foldable stroller per child is carried free of charge on all JAL routes, in addition to the normal baggage allowance. Strollers can be checked in at the gate. Car seats and baby carriers are also free.

Sports Equipment & Musical Instruments

Japan Airlines accepts sports equipment and musical instruments on most routes.

Sports Equipment

  • Types accepted: Golf bags, skis/snowboards, diving equipment, bicycles, fishing rods, etc.
  • Counts toward allowance: Most sports equipment counts as one checked bag toward your free allowance
  • Size/weight limits: Standard baggage limits apply (203 cm, 45 kg max)
  • Surfboards / windsurfing: Special fee of ~USD 200 per bag on long-haul flights, not counted in free allowance
  • Bicycles: Must be properly packed / boxed; counts as one bag
  • Proper packing required: Must be in a suitable protective case

Musical Instruments

  • Small instruments: Can be carried as cabin baggage if within 115 cm total dimensions and weight limits
  • Medium instruments: Can travel in the cabin if you buy an extra seat
  • Large instruments: Must be checked in as hold baggage
  • Domestic flights: JAL offers loaner hard cases for some instruments
  • Fragile items: Must be properly packed; JAL is not liable for damage to fragile items

Surfboard fee: Surfboards and windsurfing equipment on JAL international flights incur a special fee of approximately USD 200 per bag and are NOT counted toward your free baggage allowance. This is in addition to any overweight or oversize fees.

Other Special Items

  • Wheelchairs & mobility aids: Carried free of charge, in addition to your baggage allowance
  • Medical equipment & medication: Allowed in addition to carry-on, with proper documentation
  • Duty-free purchases: Must fit within your carry-on allowance
  • Wedding dress: Can be carried on board if it fits within cabin bag limits, or checked in
  • Assistive devices: Priority handling for passengers with reduced mobility
  • Fragile items: Must be properly packed; limited liability for damage

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Japan Airlines' carry-on baggage allowance? ▼

Japan Airlines (JAL) allows 1 carry-on bag plus 1 personal item on all flights.

  • Carry-on bag: 55 × 40 × 25 cm (115 cm total linear dimensions)
  • Personal item: Handbag, laptop bag, or small backpack
  • Total combined weight: 10 kg (22 lbs) maximum for both items

Important: JAL is known for strictly enforcing carry-on weight limits. The 10 kg combined limit is often checked at the gate, especially on busy flights. If your bags are overweight, you will be required to check them and pay excess baggage fees at the airport rate.

Personal items allowed include: handbag, laptop bag, small backpack, camera bag, coat, umbrella, reading material, infant food, and mobility aids.

How much checked baggage does Japan Airlines allow? ▼

Japan Airlines has two completely different baggage systems depending on your route:

Domestic (Japan) — weight concept:

  • Economy / Class J: 20 kg total (unlimited number of bags)
  • First Class: 45 kg total
  • Max per bag: 32 kg
  • Size: 50 × 60 × 120 cm

International — piece concept:

  • Economy / Premium Economy: 2 pieces × 23 kg
  • Business / First: 3 pieces × 32 kg
  • Max per bag: 45 kg / 203 cm total dimensions

Note: If your domestic flight is included in an international ticket, international piece-concept rules apply to the domestic segment as well.

How much does excess baggage cost on Japan Airlines? ▼

JAL excess baggage fees depend on your route and the type of excess:

International flights:

  • Additional bag: JPY 10,000 (~USD 100) for Asia/Guam/Oceania routes, or JPY 20,000 (~USD 200) for Americas/Europe/Middle East/Africa
  • Overweight (23–32 kg): JPY 10,000 (~USD 100) per piece
  • Overweight (32–45 kg): JPY 60,000 (~USD 600) per piece
  • Oversize (over 203 cm): JPY 20,000 (~USD 200) per piece
  • Maximum extra bags: 7 additional pieces

Important: Fees stack! A bag that is extra AND overweight AND oversize incurs all three charges.

Domestic flights: Excess is charged per kilogram. Rates vary by route.

What is JAL baggage allowance for domestic flights in Japan? ▼

Japan Airlines domestic flights use a weight concept system:

  • Economy Class / Class J: 20 kg total free checked baggage
  • First Class: 45 kg total free checked baggage
  • Number of bags: Unlimited — as long as total weight is within your allowance
  • Maximum per bag: 32 kg
  • Size limit per bag: 50 × 60 × 120 cm

Important exception: If your domestic Japan flight is included in an international ticket (e.g., you are connecting from an international JAL flight), international baggage rules apply to the domestic segment. You get the piece-concept allowance (2 bags × 23 kg in Economy), not the domestic 20 kg weight allowance.

Excess baggage on domestic flights is charged per kilogram at the airport.

What is Japan Airlines' infant baggage allowance? ▼

Infants (under 2 years) traveling on an adult's lap on JAL:

International flights:

  • 1 checked bag (up to 115 cm total dimensions)
  • Free additional items: 1 foldable stroller, 1 baby carrier, 1 car seat
  • Baby food, formula, and sterilized water allowed in carry-on
  • Bassinet available on request (for infants under 12 months / 10 kg)

Domestic flights:

  • No separate checked baggage allowance
  • Free stroller and car seat

Children aged 2 and over with their own seat get the full adult baggage allowance for their travel class.

Unaccompanied minor service is available for children ages 5–11 for a fee.

Does JAL weigh carry-on bags? ▼

Yes, Japan Airlines does weigh carry-on bags, and the 10 kg combined limit is enforced more strictly than on many other airlines.

JAL is known for checking carry-on weights at the gate, especially on:

  • Busy flights (especially domestic Japan routes)
  • Flights on smaller aircraft
  • Peak travel periods

If your carry-on bag is overweight or too large, you will be required to check it in and may have to pay excess baggage fees at the airport rate, which is more expensive than pre-purchasing online.

It is always better to pack within the 10 kg limit. Weigh your bags at home before heading to the airport to avoid unexpected charges.

What is the maximum weight for a single bag on JAL? ▼

45 kg (100 lbs) is the absolute maximum weight for any single checked bag on Japan Airlines international flights.

If your bag weighs more than 45 kg, you must split the contents into multiple bags. Bags over 45 kg will not be accepted as checked baggage — they must be shipped as cargo instead.

Domestic flights: Maximum per bag is 32 kg.

Note: The standard free allowance varies by class and route. On international Economy, bags are limited to 23 kg each. If your bag is 23–32 kg, you will pay an overweight fee of ~JPY 10,000 (USD 100). If it is 32–45 kg, the overweight fee jumps to ~JPY 60,000 (USD 600).

Maximum dimensions per bag: 203 cm total (length + width + height) on international routes.

Can I bring sports equipment on Japan Airlines? ▼

Yes, Japan Airlines accepts most types of sports equipment on both domestic and international flights.

Most sports equipment counts toward your standard baggage allowance:

  • Golf bags (counts as 1 bag)
  • Skis / snowboards (counts as 1 bag)
  • Diving equipment (counts as 1 bag)
  • Bicycles (must be boxed / properly packed)
  • Fishing rods

Special case — surfboards / windsurfing equipment:

  • Special fee of ~USD 200 per bag on international long-haul flights
  • NOT counted toward your free baggage allowance
  • Additional overweight/oversize fees may apply

All sports equipment must be properly packed in a suitable protective case. Standard size and weight limits apply (203 cm, 45 kg max on international routes).
