Two baggage systems: Qatar Airways uses two different baggage allowance systems depending on your route — Piece Concept (Africa & Americas) and Weight Concept (all other routes). Make sure you know which one applies to your flight.

Carry-On Baggage

Qatar Airways' carry-on allowance depends on your travel class. The rules are the same across all routes.

Class Number of Bags Max Size Weight Limit
Economy Class 1 piece 50 × 37 × 25 cm 7 kg
Business Class 2 pieces 50 × 37 × 25 cm each 15 kg total
First Class 2 pieces 50 × 37 × 25 cm each 15 kg total

Laptop rule: Laptops must fit inside your main carry-on bag. If you carry a separate laptop bag, it counts as one of your carry-on items — you cannot have both a laptop bag and a separate cabin bag in Economy. Pack your laptop inside your main bag to avoid issues.

Additional items allowed

In addition to your carry-on allowance, you may also bring:

  • A small handbag / purse
  • A coat or wrap
  • An umbrella
  • Reading material
  • Duty-free purchases (must fit within allowance)
  • Infant food for consumption on board
  • A collapsible walking stick / crutches

Brazil route exception

For flights to/from Brazil, Economy passengers may carry 1 piece up to 10 kg instead of 7 kg.

Checked Baggage: Two Systems

Qatar Airways operates two different checked baggage systems. Which one applies depends on your route:

Piece Concept
Routes: Flights to/from Africa and the Americas

You get a set number of bags, each with a maximum weight:

  • Fixed number of pieces
  • Each bag has a weight limit (23kg or 32kg)
  • Max size: 158 cm (L+W+H) per bag
  • Extra bags charged per piece
Weight Concept
Routes: All other destinations (Europe, Middle East, Asia, Oceania)

You get a total weight allowance, split across any number of bags:

  • Total weight limit (e.g., 30kg)
  • Any number of bags (within total weight)
  • Max size: 300 cm (L+W+H) per bag
  • Extra weight charged per kg

Piece Concept (Africa & Americas)

For flights to/from Africa and the Americas, baggage is counted by the piece. Each bag has a maximum weight and size limit.

Fare / Class Free Bags Weight Per Bag Max Size (per bag)
Economy Lite 1 piece 23 kg 158 cm (L+W+H)
Economy Classic / Convenience / Comfort 2 pieces 23 kg each 158 cm (L+W+H)
Business Class 2 pieces 32 kg each 158 cm (L+W+H)
First Class 2 pieces 32 kg each 158 cm (L+W+H)

Hard limit: No single bag may weigh more than 32 kg. If your bag exceeds 32 kg, you must split the contents into multiple bags. Bags over 32 kg will not be accepted as checked baggage.

Weight Concept (All Other Routes)

For all routes except Africa and the Americas, baggage is counted by total weight. You can split this weight across any number of bags.

Fare / Class Total Weight Number of Bags Max Size (per bag)
Economy Lite 20 kg Unlimited (within weight) 300 cm (L+W+H)
Economy Classic 25 kg Unlimited (within weight) 300 cm (L+W+H)
Economy Convenience 30 kg Unlimited (within weight) 300 cm (L+W+H)
Economy Comfort 35 kg Unlimited (within weight) 300 cm (L+W+H)
Business Class 40 kg Unlimited (within weight) 300 cm (L+W+H)
First Class 40 kg Unlimited (within weight) 300 cm (L+W+H)

Weight concept tip: On weight concept routes, you can bring as many bags as you like as long as the total weight stays within your allowance. This is great for families or travelers with multiple smaller bags — just make sure no single bag exceeds 32 kg.

Student Club: Extra Baggage for Students

If you are a full-time student between 18 and 30 years old, Qatar Airways Student Club can help you save on flights and carry more luggage. It is free to join and comes with several travel benefits built around student needs.

What you get: Extra baggage allowance, fare discounts, flexible date changes, free in-flight Wi-Fi, and a Privilege Club tier upgrade when you graduate — all for free as long as you verify your student status.

Extra Baggage Allowance

The extra baggage benefit depends on which baggage concept applies to your route:

  • Weight Concept routes: +10 kg of additional checked baggage on top of your standard allowance
  • Piece Concept routes: +1 additional piece of baggage (up to 23 kg in Economy, 32 kg in Business)

This extra allowance applies to you and up to 3 nominated travel companions when they fly with you.

Other Student Club Benefits

Fare Discounts

  • 1st flight: 10% off base fare
  • 2nd flight: 15% off base fare
  • 3rd & 4th flights: 20% off base fare
  • Discounts apply to published fares only

Flexible Changes

  • 2 free date changes per booking
  • For you and your companions
  • Fare difference may still apply
  • Great for uncertain schedules

Free Wi-Fi

  • Complimentary Super Wi-Fi
  • Stay connected in the air
  • Stream, study, or chat
  • Voucher with each booking

Graduation Upgrade

  • Fly 1 round-trip per year
  • Get a tier upgrade on graduation
  • Burgundy → Silver (Oneworld Ruby)
  • Submit proof within 6 months

How to Join

  1. Sign up for a free Privilege Club account on qatarairways.com
  2. Go to Student Club and fill in your details (name, university, graduation date)
  3. Verify your student status through SheerID using your student ID or enrollment letter
  4. Receive your promo code and start booking with your student benefits

Important: Student Club discounts apply only to the base fare, not to taxes and surcharges. The extra baggage allowance is added automatically when you book with your Student Club promo code. Always double-check your baggage allowance on your booking confirmation before you fly.

Infant & Child Baggage

Qatar Airways offers a baggage allowance for infants and children that varies by route type.

Infants (0–2 years, on lap)

  • Piece Concept (Africa/Americas): 1 piece up to 23 kg, max 115 cm
  • Weight Concept (other routes): 10 kg total
  • 1 collapsible stroller / carrycot / pushchair — free, separate from allowance
  • No carry-on allowance — baby items must fit in the adult's carry-on bag
  • Car seat: Allowed only if the infant has a separate seat booked

Children (2–11 years, own seat)

  • Full adult baggage allowance — same as adults in the same travel class
  • Same carry-on and checked baggage benefits

Good to know: Strollers can be taken to the boarding gate and are then placed in the hold free of charge. On long-haul flights, Qatar Airways offers baby bassinets for infants — request them in advance (subject to availability).

Sports Equipment & Musical Instruments

Qatar Airways accepts sports equipment and musical instruments as part of your checked baggage allowance.

Sports Equipment

  • Within free allowance: If your sports equipment fits within your free baggage allowance (weight or pieces), it is carried at no extra charge
  • Exceeding allowance: Items exceeding your free allowance are charged at special sports equipment rates when purchased in advance
  • At the airport: Regular excess baggage rates apply (more expensive)
  • Common items: Golf clubs, skis, snowboards, surfboards, bicycles, diving equipment
  • Advance purchase: Must be booked at least 6 hours before departure for the sports rate

Musical Instruments

  • Small instruments: Can be carried as part of your cabin baggage allowance if they fit within the size and weight limits
  • Larger instruments: Can be transported in the cabin on an extra seat — book under "EXTRA ITEM SEAT"
  • Checked instruments: Can be checked in as part of your baggage allowance; must be properly packed in a hard case
  • Packing tip: Use original packaging with foam padding or a hard-shell case to prevent damage

Advance registration: Always register sports equipment and large musical instruments with Qatar Airways in advance (at least 6 hours before departure). This ensures space is available and you get the discounted sports equipment rate.

Other Special Items

  • Wheelchairs & mobility aids: Carried free of charge, in addition to your baggage allowance
  • Medical equipment & medication: Allowed in addition to carry-on, with proper documentation
  • Duty-free purchases: Must fit within your carry-on allowance
  • Sporting weapons & ammunition: May be carried as checked baggage with prior approval and proper documentation

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Qatar Airways' carry-on baggage allowance? ▼

Qatar Airways' carry-on allowance depends on your travel class:

  • Economy Class: 1 piece, max 50 × 37 × 25 cm, up to 7 kg
  • Business / First Class: 2 pieces, max 50 × 37 × 25 cm each, total 15 kg

Important: Laptops must fit inside your main carry-on bag. A separate laptop bag counts as one of your carry-on items — so in Economy, you cannot have both a laptop bag and a separate suitcase. Pack your laptop inside your main bag.

For flights to/from Brazil, Economy passengers get 10 kg instead of 7 kg.

What is the difference between piece concept and weight concept? ▼

Qatar Airways uses two different baggage systems:

  • Piece Concept: Applies to flights to/from Africa and the Americas. You get a set number of bags (pieces), each with a maximum weight (23kg in Economy, 32kg in Business). Extra bags are charged per piece.
  • Weight Concept: Applies to all other routes (Europe, Middle East, Asia, etc.). You get a total weight allowance (e.g., 25kg, 30kg) that you can split across any number of bags. Extra weight is charged per kilogram.

Always check which concept applies to your specific route before packing.

How much checked baggage does Qatar Airways allow? ▼

Qatar Airways' checked baggage allowance depends on your fare class and route:

Piece Concept (Africa/Americas):

  • Economy Lite: 1 piece × 23 kg
  • Economy Classic/Convenience/Comfort: 2 pieces × 23 kg each
  • Business / First: 2 pieces × 32 kg each

Weight Concept (other routes):

  • Economy Lite: 20 kg total
  • Economy Classic: 25 kg total
  • Economy Convenience: 30 kg total
  • Economy Comfort: 35 kg total
  • Business / First: 40 kg total
How much does extra baggage cost on Qatar Airways? ▼

Extra baggage fees depend on the route and when you purchase:

Piece Concept routes:

  • Additional 23kg bag: $130 – $255 (prepaid online)
  • Additional 32kg bag: $205 – $345 (prepaid online)
  • Overweight (23–32kg): $75 – $90

Weight Concept routes:

  • Extra weight: approximately $25 – $55 per kg, depending on route

Purchasing online in advance (at least 6 hours before departure) is significantly cheaper than paying at the airport. Use Qatar Airways' excess baggage calculator for exact prices for your route.

What is Qatar Airways' infant baggage allowance? ▼

Infants (under 2 years) traveling on an adult's lap are entitled to:

  • Piece Concept (Africa/Americas): 1 piece up to 23 kg, max 115 cm
  • Weight Concept (other routes): 10 kg total weight
  • 1 collapsible stroller / carrycot / pushchair — free, separate from baggage allowance

Important: Infants do NOT get their own carry-on baggage allowance. All baby items (diapers, bottles, food) must fit within the accompanying adult's carry-on allowance.

Infants with their own booked seat get the full adult baggage allowance.

Can I bring a laptop bag plus a carry-on on Qatar Airways? ▼

No — not in Economy Class. Qatar Airways counts a laptop bag as one of your carry-on items. If you carry a separate laptop bag, it uses up your single carry-on allowance in Economy.

To avoid issues, pack your laptop inside your main carry-on bag (the 50 × 37 × 25 cm bag). That way you have one bag that includes everything, and you can also bring a small personal item like a handbag.

In Business and First Class, you get 2 carry-on items, so you could have a laptop bag plus another bag.

Does Qatar Airways weigh carry-on bags? ▼

Yes, Qatar Airways does weigh carry-on bags at many airports, especially at check-in and boarding gates. The 7 kg (Economy) and 15 kg (Business/First) weight limits are enforced.

If your carry-on bag is overweight or too large, you may be required to check it in and pay excess baggage fees. It is always better to pack within the limits to avoid unexpected charges at the airport.

What is the maximum weight for a single checked bag? ▼

32 kg is the absolute maximum weight for any single checked bag on Qatar Airways, regardless of class or route.

If your bag weighs more than 32 kg, you must split the contents into multiple bags. Bags over 32 kg will not be accepted as checked baggage — they must be shipped as cargo instead.

Note: In Economy Class on piece concept routes, the free allowance is 23 kg per bag. If your bag is 23–32 kg, you will pay an overweight fee of approximately $75–$90.

Does Qatar Airways Student Club give extra baggage? ▼

Yes. Qatar Airways Student Club members get extra baggage on top of their standard allowance:

  • Weight Concept routes: +10 kg additional checked baggage
  • Piece Concept routes: +1 additional piece of baggage

Other Student Club benefits include: up to 20% off fares (graduated discount), 2 free date changes, complimentary in-flight Wi-Fi, and a Privilege Club tier upgrade when you graduate (if you fly at least one round-trip per year).

Membership is free for full-time students aged 18–30. You verify your status through SheerID and receive a promo code to use when booking.
