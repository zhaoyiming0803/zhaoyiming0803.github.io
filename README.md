## About Me

My name is Yiming Zhao. I'm a senior engineer and frequent traveller. For years, I've flown all over the world for work and fun, and I thought I knew how airports work — pack light, arrive early, know the rules.

I was wrong.

## The Story

Last year, I flew Ryanair from London to Vienna for a short trip. I had my usual bag: a small rolling suitcase that had fit in overhead bins on many flights, plus a backpack for under the seat. I even checked the Ryanair website before packing. Or at least, I tried to. Their baggage page was hard to follow. It had different ticket types, extra fees, and lots of small print. I thought my bag would be fine.

It wasn't.

At the gate, a worker stopped me before I could get on the plane. She pointed at my suitcase and said it was too big. I picked up my bag and tried to push it into the metal sizer. It didn't fit. Not even close. The wheels stuck out. The handle stuck up. It was clearly too big.

She said I'd have to check it at the gate. The fee was **€95**.

My flight ticket had only cost about €30. Now I was paying three times that for a bag I really thought was allowed.

That $100 fee stayed with me long after the trip. Not because of the money, but because it was so easy to avoid. I had done my homework. I had gone to the official website. I had read what I thought were the rules. But the information was spread across many pages, hidden behind extra offers and confusing words. By the time I realized my bag was too big, I was already at the gate.

I started wondering how many other travellers were getting hit with these fees. And why was it so hard to find a clear, simple answer to a basic question: **what am I actually allowed to bring on the plane?**

So I built [Luggage Validator](https://luggagevalidator.com).

## Luggage Validator

[Luggage Validator](https://luggagevalidator.com) is a free website that provides clear, up-to-date baggage rules for most airlines, plus a baggage limit checker tool that automatically calculates the maximum size and weight you can bring on your journey.

### Features

- **177 airlines** covered and growing
- **Baggage calculator** — check exact size & weight limits for your route and cabin class
- **Weekly updates** — kept in sync with official airline websites
- **Real traveller stories** — 510+ firsthand experiences from airport encounters
- **Official sources** — every page links directly to the airline's official website

### Why It Exists

Airline official websites have extensive content, but it's often hard to find the specific information you need right away. Luggage Validator cuts through the noise and gives you clear, actionable answers.
