Two route systems: Aer Lingus has different baggage rules for European/short-haul flights and transatlantic/long-haul flights. Make sure you know which applies to your journey — the allowances, fees, and fare types are different.

Carry-On Baggage

All Aer Lingus passengers can bring a small personal item on board for free. The larger cabin bag allowance depends on your fare type and route.

Item Max Size Weight Limit Notes
Small personal item 40 × 30 × 20 cm No weight limit Free on all fares
Cabin bag (10kg) 55 × 40 × 24 cm 10 kg Free on transatlantic & regional; fee on European Saver

European Flights: Cabin Bag Rules

On European flights, the cabin bag policy depends on your fare:

  • Saver fare: You can check your 10kg cabin bag for free at the airport, but to bring it into the cabin you must pay a fee (~€9.99 / £9.99 per flight)
  • Plus / Advantage / AerSpace fares: Cabin bag is included free of charge
  • AerClub Silver/Gold/Platinum members: Cabin bag is included free

Regional flights (ATR aircraft): On smaller regional planes operated by ATR, the cabin bag size limit is reduced to 48 × 33 × 20 cm with a 7 kg weight limit. Your larger bag will be gate-checked and returned to you at the aircraft door on arrival.

Additional items allowed

In addition to your carry-on allowance, you may also bring:

  • A coat or wrap
  • An umbrella
  • Reading material
  • Duty-free purchases (must fit within allowance)
  • Infant food for consumption on board
  • A walking stick / crutches / mobility aids
  • A small camera or binoculars

Checked Baggage: European vs Transatlantic

Aer Lingus operates two different baggage systems depending on your route. The allowances, fare types, and fees are different.

European / Short-Haul
Routes: Flights within Europe, UK, Ireland

Baggage is purchased by weight (20kg / 25kg / 30kg):

  • Saver fare: no free checked bag
  • Plus / Advantage: 1 × 20 kg included
  • AerSpace: 1 × 20 kg included
  • Max per bag: 32 kg
  • Max size: 158 cm (L+W+H)
Transatlantic / Long-Haul
Routes: Flights to/from USA, Canada

Baggage is counted by the piece (each up to 23kg):

  • Economy Saver: 0 bags
  • Economy Smart / Flex: 1 × 23 kg
  • Business Class: 3 × 23 kg (69 kg total)
  • Max per bag: 32 kg
  • Max size: 158 cm (L+W+H)

Lost, Damaged & Delayed Baggage

If your baggage is lost, damaged, or delayed during your Aer Lingus flight, you have rights under the Montreal Convention and Aer Lingus' own policies. Here is what you need to know.

Your rights: All international Aer Lingus flights are covered by the Montreal Convention, which sets a liability limit of approximately 1,288 SDR (Special Drawing Rights) per passenger — roughly €1,885 / $1,700 depending on exchange rates. This covers lost, damaged, and delayed baggage.

Delayed Baggage

If your bag does not arrive with you, report it immediately at the airport. Aer Lingus will provide interim expense reimbursement for essentials like toiletries and clothing.

Period Average Daily Allowance Notes
Day 1–3 ~€60 / day Higher rate for first few days
Day 4–8 ~€40 / day Medium rate
Day 8–21 ~€20 / day Lower rate after first week
  • Report at airport: Go to the Aer Lingus baggage desk before leaving the airport — you will get a PIR (Property Irregularity Report) number
  • Report online: If you already left, report within 5 days via WorldTracer
  • Claim deadline: 21 days from the date you receive your delayed bag
  • Keep receipts: All expenses must be backed up by original receipts

Damaged Baggage

If your baggage arrives damaged, Aer Lingus will arrange for repair or replacement.

  • Report at airport: Report damage at the information desk when you arrive — best option
  • Report online: If you notice damage later, report within 7 days (Montreal Convention) or 5 days via Aer Lingus WorldTracer
  • Take photos: Always photograph the damage before leaving the airport
  • Keep your bag: Do not throw away the damaged bag — you may need it for inspection

Lost Baggage

If your bag is not found within 21 days, it is considered lost and you can file a claim for compensation.

  • 21-day rule: Baggage is officially declared "lost" after 21 days of tracing
  • Compensation limit: Up to ~1,288 SDR (≈ €1,885 / $1,700) under Montreal Convention
  • Claim deadline: Submit your written claim within 21 days
  • What you need: List of contents, value, receipts, PIR number, baggage tracing questionnaire
  • Baggage fee refund: You are also entitled to a refund of any baggage fees paid

How to File a Claim

  1. Report immediately: Go to the Aer Lingus baggage service desk at the airport and get a PIR number
  2. Track online: Use the WorldTracer reference number to check status online
  3. Fill out questionnaire: Complete the Baggage Tracing Questionnaire with detailed contents of your bag
  4. Submit documents: Send receipts, photos, and any other supporting documents
  5. Wait for assessment: Aer Lingus will review your claim and respond with a decision
  6. Payment: If approved, reimbursement is typically processed in 5–10 business days via bank transfer

Time limits are strict: Miss the deadline and Aer Lingus can legally reject your claim. Report damage within 7 days and delayed/lost baggage within 21 days. Always get your PIR number at the airport before leaving.

Infant & Child Baggage

Aer Lingus offers baggage allowances for infants and children that vary by route type.

Infants (0–2 years, on lap)

  • European routes: No free checked baggage
  • Transatlantic routes: 1 checked bag up to 10 kg
  • 1 collapsible stroller / pushchair — free on all routes, can be taken to boarding gate
  • 1 car seat OR travel cot — free on all routes
  • No carry-on allowance — baby items must fit in the adult's carry-on bag

Children (2–11 years, own seat)

  • Full adult baggage allowance — same as adults in the same travel class
  • Same carry-on and checked baggage benefits
  • 1 collapsible stroller also allowed free of charge

Good to know: Strollers can be taken to the boarding gate and are then placed in the hold free of charge. You can pick them up at the baggage claim or at the aircraft door on arrival, depending on the airport.

Sports Equipment & Musical Instruments

Aer Lingus accepts sports equipment and musical instruments, with different rules for European and transatlantic routes.

Sports Equipment

  • European flights: 1 item per passenger, max 23 kg, approximately €40 online (€50 at airport)
  • Transatlantic flights: 1 sports item included in free baggage allowance; additional items cost ~€75 each
  • Bicycles: Must be booked in advance by phone, approximately €50 each way
  • Common items: Golf clubs, skis, snowboards, surfboards, diving equipment, bicycles
  • Packing: Must be in a suitable protective case or bag

Musical Instruments

  • Small instruments: Can be carried as cabin baggage if they fit within the size and weight limits
  • Medium instruments: Up to 94 × 58 × 41 cm — can travel in the cabin if you buy an extra seat ("EXTRA ITEM SEAT")
  • Large instruments: Must be checked in as hold baggage, standard baggage rates apply
  • Checked instrument fee: ~€40 online / €50 at airport on European routes
  • Packing tip: Use a hard-shell case or original packaging with padding to prevent damage

Advance booking required: Always register sports equipment and large musical instruments with Aer Lingus in advance. For bicycles and other large items, you must call to book — you cannot add them online.

Other Special Items

  • Wheelchairs & mobility aids: Carried free of charge, in addition to your baggage allowance
  • Medical equipment & medication: Allowed in addition to carry-on, with proper documentation
  • Duty-free purchases: Must fit within your carry-on allowance
  • Urn carrying ashes: Allowed as carry-on or checked baggage, with proper documentation
  • Wedding dress: Can be carried on board if it fits within the cabin bag size limit, or checked in

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Aer Lingus' carry-on baggage allowance? ▼

Aer Lingus allows two items of carry-on baggage:

  • 1 small personal item: 40 × 30 × 20 cm — free on all fares, must fit under the seat in front
  • 1 cabin bag: 55 × 40 × 24 cm, up to 10 kg

Important: On European flights with a Saver fare, you must pay a fee (~€9.99/£9.99) to bring the cabin bag into the cabin. Alternatively, you can check it for free at the airport. On transatlantic and regional flights, the cabin bag is free for all fares.

On regional ATR aircraft, the cabin bag size is reduced to 48 × 33 × 20 cm (7 kg), and larger bags are gate-checked.

How much checked baggage does Aer Lingus allow? ▼

Aer Lingus' checked baggage allowance depends on your route and fare type:

European / Short-Haul routes:

  • Saver: 0 free bags (buy 20/25/30 kg extra)
  • Plus / Advantage / AerSpace: 1 × 20 kg

Transatlantic / Long-Haul routes:

  • Economy Saver: 0 bags
  • Economy Smart / Flex: 1 × 23 kg
  • Business Class: 3 × 23 kg (69 kg total)

All bags must not exceed 158 cm (L+W+H) and 32 kg maximum weight per bag.

How much does extra baggage cost on Aer Lingus? ▼

Extra baggage fees depend on the route and when you purchase:

European routes (Saver fare, online prices):

  • 20 kg: ~€24.99–€44.99 (near/mid/far routes)
  • 25 kg: ~€29.99–€54.99
  • 30 kg: ~€34.99–€64.99
  • Airport prices are significantly higher

Transatlantic routes:

  • Additional 23 kg bag: ~€75 / $100
  • Overweight (23–32 kg): €75 / £65 / $100

Always add baggage online before your flight — it is much cheaper than paying at the airport. You can add baggage up to 2 hours before departure.

What is Aer Lingus' infant baggage allowance? ▼

Infants (under 2 years) traveling on an adult's lap are entitled to:

European routes: No free checked baggage

Transatlantic routes: 1 checked bag up to 10 kg

Free on all routes:

  • 1 collapsible stroller / pushchair
  • 1 car seat OR travel cot

Important: Infants do NOT get their own carry-on baggage allowance. All baby items (diapers, bottles, food) must fit within the accompanying adult's carry-on allowance.

Children aged 2 and over with their own seat get the full adult baggage allowance.

Can I bring sports equipment on Aer Lingus? ▼

Yes, Aer Lingus accepts sports equipment on both European and transatlantic flights:

European flights:

  • 1 item per passenger, max 23 kg
  • ~€40 online, €50 at the airport
  • Bicycles: €50, must be booked by phone

Transatlantic flights:

  • 1 sports item included in free baggage allowance
  • Additional items: ~€75 each

All sports equipment must be properly packed in a suitable protective case. Always book in advance, especially for bicycles and large items.

Does Aer Lingus weigh carry-on bags? ▼

Yes, Aer Lingus does weigh and measure carry-on bags at many airports, especially at check-in and boarding gates. The 10 kg weight limit and 55 × 40 × 24 cm size limit are enforced.

If your carry-on bag is overweight or too large, you may be required to check it in and pay excess baggage fees. On European Saver fares, if you haven't paid for the cabin bag, you will be charged at the gate (which is more expensive than paying online).

It is always better to pack within the limits to avoid unexpected charges at the airport.

What is the maximum weight for a single checked bag? ▼

32 kg is the absolute maximum weight for any single checked bag on Aer Lingus, regardless of class or route.

If your bag weighs more than 32 kg, you must split the contents into multiple bags. Bags over 32 kg will not be accepted as checked baggage — they must be shipped as cargo instead.

Note: The standard free allowance is 20 kg (European) or 23 kg (transatlantic Economy). If your bag is 23–32 kg, you will pay an overweight fee of €75 / £65 / $100.

Can I bring a musical instrument on Aer Lingus? ▼

Yes, you can bring musical instruments on Aer Lingus flights:

  • Small instruments: Can be carried as cabin baggage if they fit within the 55 × 40 × 24 cm size and 10 kg weight limit
  • Medium instruments: Up to 94 × 58 × 41 cm — can travel in the cabin if you buy an extra seat (book as "EXTRA ITEM SEAT")
  • Large instruments: Must be checked in as hold baggage; standard baggage rates apply

On European routes, checked musical instruments cost approximately €40 online (€50 at airport). Always use a hard-shell case to protect your instrument.

What happens if Aer Lingus loses my baggage? ▼

If Aer Lingus loses your baggage, you are entitled to compensation under the Montreal Convention:

  • Liability limit: Approximately 1,288 SDR (≈ €1,885 / $1,700) per passenger
  • Declared lost: After 21 days of unsuccessful tracing
  • Claim deadline: Submit written claim within 21 days
  • Refund of fees: You also get your baggage fees refunded

What to do: Report immediately at the airport baggage desk, get a PIR number, fill out the baggage tracing questionnaire, and keep all receipts. Claims are typically processed in 5–10 business days after approval.

How do I claim for delayed or damaged baggage on Aer Lingus? ▼

Delayed baggage:

  • Report at the airport baggage desk before leaving — get a PIR number
  • If already left, report within 5 days online via WorldTracer
  • Aer Lingus reimburses interim expenses: ~€60/day (days 1-3), ~€40/day (days 4-8), ~€20/day (days 8-21)
  • Claim within 21 days of receiving your bag

Damaged baggage:

  • Report damage at the airport immediately — best option
  • If noticed later, report within 7 days (Montreal Convention)
  • Take photos of the damage before leaving the airport
  • Keep the damaged bag for inspection

Important: Always keep all receipts and your PIR number. Time limits are strict — miss the deadline and your claim can be rejected.
