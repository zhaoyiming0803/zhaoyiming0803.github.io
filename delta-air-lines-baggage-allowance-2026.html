Carry-On Baggage

Delta Air Lines allows 1 carry-on bag + 1 personal item for all passengers, including those flying Basic Economy. This is a key difference from United and American Airlines, which restrict Basic Economy to a personal item only.

Carry-On Size & Weight

Item Size (inches) Size (cm) Weight
Carry-On Bag 22 × 14 × 9 in 56 × 35 × 23 cm No published limit (domestic)
Personal Item 17 × 13 × 8 in 43 × 33 × 20 cm Not specified

Note: Carry-on size includes handles and wheels. Delta does not typically weigh carry-on bags on domestic US flights — the main requirement is that the bag fits in the overhead bin and you can lift it safely.

What Counts as a Personal Item?

  • Purse, small backpack, or laptop bag
  • Must fit completely under the seat in front of you
  • Diaper bags (for passengers with infants)
  • Camera bags
  • Pet carriers (counts as either personal item or carry-on)

Basic Economy Carry-On Rules

Unlike some other US carriers, Delta Basic Economy includes a full-size carry-on bag. However, there are some caveats:

  • Basic Economy passengers board in the last group
  • Overhead bin space may be limited by the time you board
  • If no bin space is available, your bag will be gate-checked for free
  • You still get a personal item that goes under the seat

Checked Baggage: Domestic vs International

Delta has different baggage rules and fees depending on whether you're flying domestically or internationally. The key difference is that on most international routes, Main Cabin passengers get the first checked bag for free.

Extra & Excess Baggage Fees

If your bags exceed the weight or size limits, Delta charges additional fees on top of the standard checked bag fee. These fees are per bag, per direction, and they stack — a bag that's both overweight AND oversized pays both fees.

Gate Check Fees & Rules

Delta is generally considered more lenient with gate checks compared to budget carriers like Spirit or Frontier. Here's what you need to know about fees at the gate:

Good news: Delta does not charge a punitive gate check fee like European budget airlines. If your carry-on needs to be checked at the gate because overhead bins are full, it's usually free.

When Gate Checking Happens

  • Full flights: When overhead bins run out of space, gate agents will ask for volunteers to gate-check bags (free)
  • Regional jets: Smaller aircraft (CRJ, Embraer) often have smaller bins — larger roll-aboards get gate-checked at no cost
  • Basic Economy: Last to board, so higher chance bins are full — but gate check is still free
  • Oversized bags: If your bag clearly doesn't fit the sizer, you may be asked to check it and pay the standard checked bag fee

What About Actual Fees at the Gate?

Delta doesn't have a separate "gate fee" like Wizz Air or Ryanair's €65 charge. However, there are some situations where you might pay extra at the gate:

  • Oversized carry-on: If your bag is clearly too big and you haven't paid for it, you'll pay the standard checked bag fee ($45 domestic first bag)
  • Extra bags: If you show up with more bags than allowed, you'll pay standard bag fees at the gate
  • Special items: Sports equipment, musical instruments, etc. may have gate fees if not pre-arranged

Pro tip: To avoid any gate issues, make sure your carry-on fits the 22×14×9 inch sizer. If you're flying Basic Economy on a full flight, consider checking your bag in advance to avoid the hassle at the gate — it costs the same either way on Delta.

Infant & Child Baggage

Delta offers special baggage allowances for infants and children, though the rules differ between domestic and international travel.

Infants (Under 2 Years Old)

On lap (no seat, 10% of adult fare):

  • Domestic flights: No free checked baggage allowance
  • International flights: 1 checked bag up to 20 lbs (10 kg), max 45 inches total dimensions
  • Free items (all flights):
    • 1 fully collapsible stroller (check at gate or counter, free)
    • 1 car seat (FAA approved, can use on board if seat available or check free)
    • 1 bassinet / infant seat
    • Breast pump and cooler bag

Children (2-11 Years Old)

Children with their own ticket (paid fare) receive the same baggage allowance as adults in the same cabin class. This applies to both carry-on and checked bags.

Good to know: Strollers and car seats never count against your baggage allowance on Delta. You can check them for free at the ticket counter, curbside, or at the gate.

Musical Instruments

Delta is fairly generous with musical instruments:

  • Small instruments (guitar, violin, flute, etc.): Can be carried on as your free carry-on bag, as long as they fit in the overhead bin
  • Larger instruments: Can be checked as regular baggage (standard fees apply)
  • Fragile instruments: You can purchase an extra seat for the instrument (must meet size/weight requirements)
  • Checked instrument limits: Up to 165 lbs (75 kg), 150 inches (381 cm) total dimensions

Note: The standard 22×14×9 inch carry-on size limit does not strictly apply to small musical instruments — the test is whether it physically fits in the overhead bin, not exact measurements.

Other Special Items

Wheelchairs & Mobility Aids

Wheelchairs, walkers, canes, and other mobility aids fly for free and do not count toward your baggage allowance. You can gate-check a wheelchair and pick it up at the gate upon arrival.

Medical Equipment

Required medical devices and supplies are allowed in addition to your carry-on allowance, at no extra charge. This includes CPAP machines, oxygen concentrators, and other necessary medical equipment.

Pets

Small pets can travel in the cabin for a fee (usually $95 each way for domestic flights). The pet carrier counts as either your carry-on bag or personal item. Larger pets must travel as checked baggage or cargo.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is Delta's checked baggage fee? ▼

For domestic US/Canada flights, Delta charges $45 for the first checked bag and $55 for the second checked bag (each way). For international flights, the first checked bag is usually free for Main Cabin passengers, but Basic Economy passengers may pay $60-$75. Excess, overweight, and oversized bags cost extra. Use our Delta baggage calculator for exact fees for your route.

What is Delta's carry-on size limit? ▼

Delta's carry-on bag size limit is 22 x 14 x 9 inches (56 x 35 x 23 cm), including handles and wheels. You also get one personal item (17 x 13 x 8 inches) that must fit under the seat in front of you. Unlike some other US carriers, Delta includes a free carry-on bag even for Basic Economy passengers.

Does Delta charge a gate check fee for carry-on bags? ▼

Delta generally does not charge a separate gate check fee. If overhead bin space is full, gate agents will gate-check your carry-on bag for free. However, if your bag is clearly oversized and you're in Basic Economy, you may be charged the standard checked bag fee. Delta is considered more lenient than budget carriers like Spirit or Frontier when it comes to gate checks.

How strict is Delta with carry-on weight? ▼

Delta does not have a published weight limit for carry-on bags on domestic US flights. The main requirement is that the bag fits in the overhead bin and you can lift it into the bin yourself. On some international routes, there may be weight restrictions, but Delta is generally less strict about carry-on weight compared to Asian or European carriers.

What is the maximum weight for a checked bag on Delta? ▼

The maximum weight for a checked bag on Delta is 50 lbs (23 kg) for Economy and Comfort+ passengers. First Class, Delta One, and Premium Select passengers get a 70 lbs (32 kg) limit. Bags over 50 lbs (or 70 lbs in premium cabins) incur overweight fees: $100 for 51-70 lbs and $200 for 71-100 lbs. Bags over 100 lbs are not accepted.

Does Basic Economy include a carry-on on Delta? ▼

Yes, Delta Basic Economy includes a free carry-on bag plus a personal item, unlike United and American Basic Economy which only allow a personal item. However, Basic Economy passengers board last, so overhead bin space may be limited and your carry-on might need to be gate-checked (which is free on Delta).

How can I calculate my Delta baggage fees? ▼

The calculator takes into account domestic vs international routes, different fare types, overweight and oversized fees, and more. It's completely free to use.

What's the difference between domestic and international baggage on Delta? ▼

On domestic US/Canada flights, all economy passengers pay for checked bags ($45 first bag, $55 second). On most international routes (Europe, Asia, Australia), Main Cabin passengers get the first checked bag free, and the second bag costs $100. Basic Economy passengers on international routes may still pay for the first bag ($60-$75 depending on the region).
